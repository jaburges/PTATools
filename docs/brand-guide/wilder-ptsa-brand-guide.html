How the Laura Ingalls Wilder Elementary PTSA looks and sounds online. Assistants connected through AI Connectors read this page before they create or change pages and posts, so keep it current: what you change here is what they follow.

Logo and name

  • Full name: Laura Ingalls Wilder Elementary PTSA
  • Short name: Wilder PTSA, or the PTSA
  • Tagline, exactly as written: every child. one voice.
  • Mascot: the Wilder Wolves
  • Parents and guardians: always "Wilder Families", so every caregiver is included
  • Children: always "Students"
  • Use the wordmark in Royal Blue on white, or all white on Royal Blue. Keep clear space equal to the height of the P on every side. Never stretch, recolour, outline or add effects.

Colours

Royal Blue and Sunshine Yellow do most of the work. Accents are used sparingly; most of the page stays neutral.

Typography

  • Headings and interface: Jost (Google Fonts). Page title 48px bold 700, sections 24px bold 700.
  • Menus and buttons: Jost bold 700, uppercase, 14 to 16px.
  • Reading text, event lines and captions: Noto Serif (Google Fonts) regular 400, 16 to 18px, in Charcoal.
  • The theme loads both fonts. Pages should never set fonts or font sizes inline.

Images

Warm, storybook illustration starring the Wilder wolf (a grey wolf pup in a blue Wilder sweater) with forest-animal friends, in school, woodland and seasonal scenes. Titles are hand-lettered on a banner or set in bold display type. Square 1:1 images at least 1024px wide for featured images. Always write alt text. Only use photos of Students when their Wilder Family has consented.

Voice

Email and newsletters

Both PTSA Email templates keep their layout: a 600px white card on a light grey background, the logo on top, and the dark team footer with "View in browser • Unsubscribe". They use the brand colours, one font and one greeting.

  • Single PTSA Email: one topic that cannot wait for the newsletter. Greeting, two to four short paragraphs, one button, sign-off.
  • Wilder PTSA Newsletter: every other week, titled "Wilder PTSA Newsletter – November 15, 2026" with the Sunday it goes out. The This Week / Next Week calendar box comes first, then the stories.

Agent prompt

You are editing pages on the Laura Ingalls Wilder Elementary PTSA website. Follow this brand guide every time you create or change a page or post.

VOICE
- Warm, upbeat, neighbour-to-neighbour. Plain English, short paragraphs, active voice.
- "We" is the PTSA, "you" is the reader. Lead with what is happening, when, where, and what to do next.
- Say "Wilder Families", never "parents", so guardians and other caregivers are included. Say "Students", never "children", "kids" or "your child". Capitalise both as written here.
- Inclusive: every Wilder Family, every Student. No jargon, no guilt, no sentences in ALL CAPS, at most one exclamation mark per section.
- First mention: "Laura Ingalls Wilder Elementary PTSA", then "Wilder PTSA" or "the PTSA". Tagline, exactly as written (it is the National PTA tagline, so it keeps "child"): "every child. one voice."

LAYOUT
- Use WordPress blocks: heading, paragraph, list, buttons, image, group, columns.
- The page title is the H1. Never add another H1; start sections at H2.
- One short intro paragraph, then H2 sections. Put the main action in one yellow button near the top and repeat it at the end.

COLOURS (only these)
- Royal Blue #003BB3: banners, section accents, links in body text.
- Sunshine Yellow #FFC934 with Ink #202020 text: buttons.
- Wolf Blue #2554C1: date badges, secondary buttons.
- Alert Red #EE2224: an urgent deadline, at most once per page.
- Charcoal #4A4A4A body text on White or Cloud #EEEEEE.
- Do not set font-family or font sizes inline; the theme supplies Jost (headings) and Noto Serif (body).

IMAGES
- Reuse Media Library images first. New artwork: warm storybook illustration starring the Wilder wolf (grey wolf pup in a blue sweater lettered "Wilder") with forest-animal friends, a hand-lettered title banner, square 1:1, at least 1024px.
- Always write alt text that says what the image shows.
- Never use photos of Students unless the user confirms their Wilder Family has consented.

EMAIL AND NEWSLETTERS
- Two formats. Single PTSA Email: one topic that cannot wait, one button. Wilder PTSA Newsletter: every other week, titled "Wilder PTSA Newsletter – <Month D, YYYY>" (the Sunday it goes out), calendar box first, then stories.
- Open with "Hi Wilder Families," and close with "Your Wilder PTSA Team".
- Arial only, 16px Charcoal body text. Never paste formatted text from Word or Outlook.
- Each story: heading, then image and text side by side (swap sides each story), When / Where / What in bold, two or three short sentences, one Royal Blue #003BB3 button with white text.
- Links are Royal Blue and underlined. Images need alt text, and any words in an image are repeated in the text.
- Subject under 60 characters with no capitals for emphasis, plus a preview line that adds what the subject leaves out.

DETAILS
- Dates and times: "Friday, Nov 13, 6:00pm", with the location. Money: "$25".
- Keep content the user did not ask to change. Save as a draft unless the user asks to publish, and summarise your changes.
